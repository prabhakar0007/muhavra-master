# 👑 Muhavra Master — Game Website

Emojis me chhupe Hindi muhavare pehchanne wala ek mazedaar quiz game. Emoji dekho, sahi muhavra bujho, levels jeeto aur Muhavra Master bano!

## 🎮 Game ke baare me

**Muhavra Master** me screen par kuch emojis dikhte hain, aur unme ek mashhoor Hindi muhavra chhupa hota hai. Char options me se sahi muhavra chunna hota hai — galat jawab par ek dil (life) kam ho jata hai. Har jawab ke baad muhavre ka matlab bhi samjhaya jata hai, taaki khel ke saath-saath seekhna bhi ho jaye.

## ✨ Khaas baatein

- 🏆 **1000 Levels** — har level me 10 sawal
- 🧩 **78 asli Hindi muhavare** — har level par naye mix me aate hain
- 📈 **Badhti mushkil** — level badhne par timer tez (30 second se 8 second tak), lives kam (3 se 1 tak) aur sawal kathin
- ⭐ **Points system** — sahi jawab, speed bonus aur streak bonus; upar ke levels me points multiplier ×10 tak
- 💡 **Lifelines** — atak jao to Hint (muhavre ka ishara) ya 50-50 (2 galat options gayab)
- 🎵 **Background music** — khelte hue halki dhun bajti rehti hai, chaaho to band bhi kar sakte ho
- 📲 **Score share** — apna score doston ko bhejkar challenge karo
- 📱 **Har device par** — mobile, tablet aur computer, seedha browser me khulta hai, koi download nahi

## 🕹️ Kaise khelein?

1. Apna level chuno aur game shuru karo.
2. Emojis dekhkar andaza lagao ki kaun sa muhavra chhupa hai.
3. 4 options me se sahi muhavra tap karo — time khatm hone se pehle!
4. Jawab ke baad muhavre ka matlab padho aur agle sawal par badho.
5. Level pura karo, stars kamao, agla level unlock karo.
