About — Muhavra Master

Ye game kya hai?

Muhavra Master ek free online game hai jisme Hindi ke mashhoor muhavare emojis ke roop me dikhaye jate hain, aur player ko sahi muhavra pehchanna hota hai. Game me 1000 levels hain aur 78 muhavaron ka bank hai, jisse har level naye sawal bante hain, har jawab ke saath muhavre ka matlab bhi samjhaya jata hai — taaki khel ke saath-saath seekhna bhi ho.

Kyun banaya gaya?

Hindi muhavare hamari bhasha ki shaan hain, lekin nayi peedhi inhe bhoolti ja rahi hai. Is game ka maksad hai muhavaron ko ek mazedaar, mobile-friendly game ke roop me sab tak pahunchaana — khaaskar students aur Hindi seekhne walon ke liye.
